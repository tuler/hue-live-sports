# Credits and licenses

The Scorelights mark combines original artwork (the TV frame, stand and color fill) with ball icons from open-source icon sets. The icons were recolored and resized; their shapes are unchanged.

| Asset | Source | Author | License | What it requires |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Football, soccer ball, basketball | game-icons.net (`american-football-ball`, `soccer-ball`, `basketball-ball`) | Delapouite | CC BY 3.0 | Visible credit to the author and a link to the license |
| Baseball | Ionicons (`baseball`) | Ionic | MIT | Keep the copyright and license notice with the files |
| Hockey puck | Fluent UI System Icons (`sport-hockey-24-filled`) | Microsoft Corporation | MIT | Keep the copyright and license notice with the files |
| Wordmark lettering | Outfit typeface, converted to outlines | The Outfit Project Authors | SIL OFL 1.1 | None for outlined artwork; keep the license if you ship the font files |

Full license texts are in `licenses/`.

## Credit line to publish

CC BY 3.0 needs a credit people can see. Put this on an About or Credits page of the site and in the iOS app's acknowledgements:

> Football, soccer ball and basketball icons by Delapouite (https://delapouite.com), from https://game-icons.net, licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Colors changed.

## Philips Hue

"Scorelights for Philips Hue" and "Works with Philips Hue" are used as plain descriptive text only. The mark contains no Philips Hue, Philips or Signify logo. The orange, pink and blue fill was chosen by the Scorelights developer and resembles the palette associated with Philips Hue; review it against Signify's developer terms before public launch.
